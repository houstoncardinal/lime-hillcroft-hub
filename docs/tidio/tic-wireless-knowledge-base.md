# TIC Wireless Electronic & Repair Center — Business Knowledge Base

TIC Wireless Electronic & Repair Center (also known as "TIC Wireless" and listed on Google as "TIC Wireless - Hillcroft") is an independent cell phone store in
Houston, Texas. It sells new unlocked phones with leasing, prepaid phone plans, Xfinity
Prepaid internet and accessories, and does phone repair and unlocking. Slogan: "Buy · Sell ·
Repair · Unlock." The team speaks English and Spanish (Se habla español).

## Phones and leasing

- Unlocked iPhones and Android phones: every iPhone from the iPhone 11 to the new iPhone 18 Pro
  and 18 Pro Max, Samsung Galaxy S phones from the S20 Ultra to the S26 Ultra, Galaxy A phones,
  Motorola and more. A phone may be new or pre-owned depending on the unit; staff confirm
  condition, storage and color. Full list with specs: https://www.ticwirelesstx.com/devices
- Leasing (lease-to-own) is available: no credit needed, bad credit is OK, options start at
  $40 down. The exact down payment and payments depend on the item and the plan the customer is
  approved for. Approval is not guaranteed.
- Lease-to-own is not a loan: the customer leases the item and makes regular payments; when the
  plan is complete (or bought out early) the item is theirs. The total cost to own is more
  than the cash price. Staff explain all terms in store before anything is signed.
- Payment schedules: weekly, biweekly or monthly payments are available.
- To apply: come to the store with a valid photo ID. The application takes a few minutes at the
  counter. Staff will say if anything else is needed.
- Early payoff: customers can ask about early-payoff options when they apply.
- Leasing is also available on game consoles (PlayStation 5, Xbox, Nintendo Switch 2),
  laptops (including MacBook Air and Windows laptops), tablets (iPad, iPad Pro, iPad mini,
  Galaxy Tab), and audio and wearables (AirPods, Galaxy Buds, smartwatches, JBL speakers).
- Leasing details page: https://www.ticwirelesstx.com/leasing
- Buy · sell · trade: customers can trade in their old phone toward a new one or sell it.

## Prepaid plans, internet and payments

- Prepaid plans from 12 carriers: T-Mobile Prepaid, Verizon Prepaid, AT&T Prepaid, Cricket,
  Simple Mobile, Tracfone, H2O Wireless, Lyca Mobile, Gen Mobile, Metro by T-Mobile, Boost Mobile
  and GoSmart. No contracts.

- Bill payments: customers can pay their phone bill in person for the major prepaid carriers,
  with no extra charges.
- International recharge (top-ups to phones abroad), including Tigo, Digicel, Movistar and
  Telcel, and more.
- Plan prices change often and depend on the carrier — customers should call or visit for
  current prices.

## Repairs

- Repairs for iPhone, Samsung and other Android phones, plus tablets, laptops and computers.
- Common repairs: cracked or broken screens, batteries, charging ports, and software problems.
- Most phone screen, battery and charging-port repairs are done the same day.
- How it works: walk in (no appointment needed) → staff check the device in front of the
  customer, explain the problem and quote the price before any work starts → most repairs are
  finished the same day.
- Repair prices depend on the exact model and the problem, so prices are quoted in store or by
  phone at (713) 339-9300.
- After a repair, the store can fit tempered glass and a case to protect the device.

## Unlocking

- TIC Wireless Electronic & Repair Center unlocks phones so customers can use them with the carrier they choose.
- Customers should bring the phone in so staff can check it.
